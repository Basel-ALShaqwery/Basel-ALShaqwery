## Research & Publication

### Social Media, Algorithmic Exposure, and Mental-Health Indicators Among Egyptian Youth

An independent study investigating associations between social-media exposure, perceived recommendation algorithms, repeated exposure to psychologically distressing content, and mental-health indicators among Egyptian youth.

**Research workflow**
- Survey design and data collection.
- Data preparation and eligibility filtering.
- Statistical analysis and exploratory machine learning.
- Interpretation of associations and study limitations.
- Manuscript preparation and publication.

**Dataset:** 135 survey responses, with 133 participants in the primary age-eligible sample.

[**Read the Research Publication on Zenodo →**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22133339)
