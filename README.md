## Research & Publications

### AI, Social Media, and Mental Health Among Egyptian Youth

I conducted research examining the relationship between AI, social media, and mental health among Egyptian youth, using survey responses from more than 125 participants.

- **Research Repository:** [Read the research on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22133339)
- **DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.22133339](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22133339)

The Zenodo record contains the research description, abstract, and publication information.

### ROOTY EMSRD

An environmental and agricultural research project investigating the effects of electromagnetic fields on soil microorganisms, nutrient availability, plant growth, and chlorophyll.

Reported experimental outcomes included increases in soil microbial activity, nutrient levels, root growth, and chlorophyll measurements.

- Research focus: Environmental science, agriculture, and experimental analysis.
- Recognition: ISEF Cairo Exhibition finalist in 2024 and 2026.
